# Corrections and versions

Version 0.1.0 is the first edition. A correction should identify the version, the affected claim and a source or reproducible calculation. Once the repository opens, please report corrections through its issue tracker. An earlier construction at any of the four orders warrants a dated priority correction. An error in the construction or certificate warrants a correction or withdrawal that names the affected orders. A later edition will identify the version it supersedes. Archived editions retain their original contents.
